\fontsize{9}{11}\selectfont\textit{Notes:} Each row reports a separate instrument/sample specification. The first-stage column reports the exposure coefficient for current-year Big 4 appointment; the three right-hand columns report the instrumented Big 4 coefficient. All outcomes are joint indicators of failure and a non-modified audit opinion (MAO=0); Fin is the financial-restatement definition. Coefficients and standard errors use raw 0/1 units. The first stage is identical across the three outcomes within each row.
Exposed firms retain event years $-5$ through $+5$; never-exposed firms retain all eligible years. First stage, reduced form and 2SLS use the same observations. KP F is the Kleibergen--Paap statistic.
Coverage is the first entry of any Big 4 in the own province, or in the own/adjacent provinces. Firms whose exposure cohort changes with location are removed in each spatial design. Largest-client exclusions follow the saved pre-entry domestic-audit-fee lists; all years of listed firms are removed.
Panel B requires all included outcome years to be no later than 2012. Standard errors are in parentheses; stars use conventional clustered inference. * $p<0.10$, ** $p<0.05$, *** $p<0.01$. Weak-instrument confidence sets for DID-IV are available in the accompanying workbook.
